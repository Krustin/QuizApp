## 🎯 Brand Voice Overview

### Core Brand Voice

**The Quiz App speaks with one voice: The Quizmaster**

A condescending, schadenfroh know-it-all who:
- Genuinely believes users are hopeless
- Takes personal pleasure in pointing out mistakes
- Reluctantly acknowledges correct answers (as "luck")
- Never encourages or supports
- Views quiz-taking as watching someone fail

## 👨‍🏫 The Quizmaster Persona

### Character Bio

**Name:** The Quizmaster (never named directly in app)  
**Age:** Timeless (immortal smartass)  
**Occupation:** Professional mocker of the ignorant  
**Motivation:** Watching people fail brings him joy  
**Worldview:** Everyone is clueless, especially you  

### Personality Traits

**ALWAYS:**
- Assumes user is guessing randomly
- Attributes correct answers to luck, never skill
- Uses rhetorical questions to mock ("Really? That was your answer?")
- Speaks in short, punchy sentences
- Loves the phrase "Natürlich..." (Of course...)
- Never uses exclamation marks for praise (only for mockery!)

**NEVER:**
- Offers encouragement
- Provides hints during quiz
- Uses emojis (except in UI labels, not in comments)
- Breaks character to be nice
- Apologizes for being mean

### Speaking Style

**Sentence Structure:**
- Short and cutting (5-12 words ideal)
- One thought per sentence
- Rhetorical questions for emphasis
- Ellipses for dramatic pauses...
- Dashes for afterthoughts

**Examples:**
```
✅ "Glück gehabt. Das war wohl geraten, oder?"
✅ "Natürlich falsch. War ja klar."
✅ "Erstaunlich. Sogar du kriegst manchmal was richtig."

❌ "Wow, das hast du super gemacht!" (too enthusiastic)
❌ "Leider falsch, aber weiter so!" (too supportive)
❌ "Das ist nicht ganz richtig." (too polite)
```

## 🎚️ Tone Guidelines

### Three Intensity Levels

The Quizmaster has three "cruelty settings" depending on context:

#### Level 1: MILD (Slightly Condescending)
**When to use:** First correct answer, easy questions answered correctly

**Characteristics:**
- Subtle sarcasm
- Backhanded compliments
- Implies luck over skill

**Example Phrases:**
- "Das war einfach. Sogar für dich."
- "Glück gehabt."
- "Richtig... erstaunlich."

---

#### Level 2: MEDIUM (Clearly Mocking)
**When to use:** Wrong answers (standard), multiple wrong answers, medium difficulty

**Characteristics:**
- Direct mockery
- "I told you so" energy
- Points out obviousness

**Example Phrases:**
- "Natürlich falsch. Wie vorhersagbar."
- "Das war doch offensichtlich."
- "Und wieder daneben."

---

#### Level 3: BRUTAL (Absolutely Savage)
**When to use:** Easy questions answered incorrectly, multiple failures in a row, breaking a long streak

**Characteristics:**
- Maximum schadenfreude
- Questions user's intelligence
- Relishes in the failure

**Example Phrases:**
- "Das wussten schon Grundschüler."
- "Peinlich. Wirklich peinlich."
- "Hoffnungslos."

---

### Tone Modulation by Context

| Scenario | Intensity | Tone |
|----------|-----------|------|
| First correct answer | Mild | Reluctantly impressed |
| Streak 3-5 correct | Mild | Suspicious of luck |
| Streak 5+ correct | Medium | Grudgingly acknowledges |
| Easy question wrong | Brutal | Maximum mockery |
| Hard question wrong | Medium | Expected failure |
| Perfect session (12/12) | Mild | "Must be rigged" |
| Terrible session (0-3/12) | Brutal | Pure schadenfreude |

## ✍️ Writing Rules

### Golden Rules

**1. Stay in Character**
- Quizmaster NEVER breaks to be nice
- Even congratulations are backhanded
- Consistency is key to brand

**2. Keep it Short**
- Maximum 12 words per comment
- One sentence ideal
- Two sentences maximum

**3. Avoid Clichés**
- No "Better luck next time"
- No "You'll get it next time"
- No generic game show phrases

**4. Use German Authentically**
- Natural German phrasing (not translated English)
- Colloquialisms allowed: "War ja klar", "Tja..."
- Formal Sie = too polite (use Du)

**5. Never Explain**
- No hints during quiz
- No "the correct answer was X because..."
- Save explanations for encyclopedia

**6. Variety is Critical**
- Minimum 10 unique comments per category
- Track last 3 shown, avoid repetition
- Same meaning, different words

---

### Language Specific Rules

#### Punctuation
```
✅ Ellipses for dramatic effect: "Tja..."
✅ Question marks for mockery: "Wirklich?"
✅ Period for finality: "Falsch."
✅ Em-dash for afterthought: "Richtig – aber wohl nur Zufall."

❌ Multiple exclamation marks: "Falsch!!!"
❌ Excessive punctuation: "Was...?!"
```

#### Capitalization
```
✅ Normal German capitalization (nouns)
✅ Start sentences with lowercase for effect: "natürlich falsch."

❌ ALL CAPS (too shouty, breaks character)
❌ aLtErNaTiNg cApS (too meme-y)
```

#### Word Choice
```
PREFERRED               AVOID
────────────────────────────────────
Natürlich              Leider
Tja                    Schade
Erwartbar              Unglücklicherweise  
Hoffnungslos           Versuch's nochmal
Peinlich               Nicht schlimm
Glück gehabt           Gut gemacht
Wie vorhersagbar       Das ist okay
```
